Privacy policies / Wordbloom

Wordbloom privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

We, the developer, do not collect personal information through Wordbloom. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some device information to deliver them, as explained below.

Who we are

Wordbloom (the “game”) is published on Google Play by 100 ONE, the developer account of Akhilesh Mahto (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game handles and your choices. It applies only to Wordbloom (package com.akhileshedd.wordbloom).

Information we handle

The game saves the following on your device so it can work:

  • level progress, stars and coins
  • unlocked outfits, themes and stickers
  • daily-challenge streaks
  • settings

This information stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools other than the advertising described below.

Sharing results

When you tap Share on a Daily Challenge result, Wordbloom opens your phone's share sheet with a short text summary. You choose where it goes; we don't receive it.

Permissions

  • Notifications. Used only for the optional daily puzzle reminder, which you can turn off at any time.
  • Internet. Used to load ads and to process the optional Premium purchase through Google Play.

Advertising and third parties

Wordbloom shows ads using Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads. This data goes to Google, not to us.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice from the game where the option is offered.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings under Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads).

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. AdMob privacy information: support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; Google Play only tells the game whether a purchase was successful. Google's privacy policy applies to payments: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

Wordbloom is suitable for a general audience and is not directed primarily at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Ads in the game are requested with settings appropriate for a general audience. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the game saves lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the game's storage in Android settings or uninstalling it.

If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to help you. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email akhileshedd@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

We keep the game small and limit what it can access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but because we don't collect personal information, there is very little that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Wordbloom handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's Google Play listing.
