Privacy policies / Ring Sort - Color Game

Ring Sort - Color Game privacy policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Ring Sort - Color Game works offline. It does not collect, send or share any personal information.

Who we are

Ring Sort - Color Game (the “game”) is published on Google Play by 100 ONE, the developer account of Akhilesh Mahto (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game handles and your choices. It applies only to Ring Sort - Color Game (package com.ringsort.game).

Information we handle

The game saves the following on your device so it can work:

  • level progress
  • best move counts
  • theme and settings

This information stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Ring Sort - Color Game does not need the internet to work. We don't operate any servers for it and don't receive any data from it.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

Third parties

Ring Sort - Color Game contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking libraries, and we don't share any information with third parties.

Children

Ring Sort - Color Game is suitable for children and takes part in Google Play's Families programme. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and contains no ads or links that lead outside the game.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the game saves lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the game's storage in Android settings or uninstalling it.

If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to help you. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email akhileshedd@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

We keep the game small and limit what it can access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but because we don't collect personal information, there is very little that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Ring Sort - Color Game handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's Google Play listing.
