Arrows Rush Out privacy policy

Effective 18 August 2026

Effective date: 18/08/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Arrows Rush Out ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"), package name com.akhileshedd.arrowsrushout , handles information when you play it.

Short version: Arrows Rush Out is a fully offline game. We do NOT collect, store, or share any personal information. The Game does not require an internet connection, does not contain ads, and does not use any analytics or tracking.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email address, phone number, physical address)
  • Account or login information (the Game has no accounts and no sign-in)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files on your device
  • Advertising identifiers or any device identifiers
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry

The Game contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

2. Information stored locally on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the Game saves your game data only on your own device, in the app's private storage. This data includes:

  • Your level progress and highest unlocked level
  • In-game coins and earned stars
  • Your settings (sound, music, haptics, grid lines, and colour theme)

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is not personal information and cannot be used to identify you. If you uninstall the Game, this locally stored data is removed by the operating system.

3. Internet and network access

Arrows Rush Out does NOT request the Internet permission and does not connect to the internet. All gameplay, level generation, sound, and saving happen entirely on your device, offline.

4. Device permissions

The Game may use your device's vibration (haptic feedback) feature to provide tactile feedback during play. This is used only for gameplay feedback, involves no data collection, and can be turned off in the Game's Settings. The Game does not request access to storage outside its own private app storage, the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other sensitive device feature.

5. Children's privacy

Arrows Rush Out is suitable for players of all ages. Because the Game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not target advertising or collect data from children or any other users. The Game is compliant with children's privacy regulations (such as COPPA and GDPR-K) by virtue of collecting no personal data.

6. Third-party services

The Game does not integrate any third-party services, advertising providers, analytics providers, or social networks. No data is shared with third parties because no data is collected.

Note: If you download the Game from the Google Play Store, Google may collect certain information as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). This collection is governed by Google and is outside our control; it is not performed by the Game itself.

7. Data security

Because the Game collects and transmits no data, there is no personal information for us to secure or for a third party to intercept. Your local game data is protected by your device's own security and the app-sandboxing provided by your operating system.

8. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if a future version were to add features such as ads, analytics, online leaderboards, or purchases). If we make changes, we will update the "Effective date" above and post the revised policy at the same location. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the Game after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

This policy applies to the Arrows Rush Out mobile application only.
