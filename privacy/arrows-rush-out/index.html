Privacy policies / Arrows Rush Out

Arrows Rush Out privacy policy

Effective 18 August 2026

Arrows Rush Out works offline. It does not collect, send or share any personal information.

Who we are

Arrows Rush Out (the “game”) is published on Google Play by 100 ONE, the developer account of Akhilesh Mahto (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game handles and your choices. It applies only to Arrows Rush Out (package com.akhileshedd.arrowsrushout).

Information we handle

The game saves the following on your device so it can work:

  • level progress
  • selected theme and settings

This information stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Arrows Rush Out does not need the internet to work and does not request internet permission, so it can't send anything off your device.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

Third parties

Arrows Rush Out contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking libraries, and we don't share any information with third parties.

Children

Arrows Rush Out is suitable for a general audience and is not directed primarily at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the game saves lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the game's storage in Android settings or uninstalling it.

If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to help you. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email akhileshedd@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

We keep the game small and limit what it can access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but because we don't collect personal information, there is very little that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Arrows Rush Out handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's Google Play listing.
