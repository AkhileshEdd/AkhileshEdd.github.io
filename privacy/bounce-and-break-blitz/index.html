Bounce & Break Blitz privacy policy

Last updated 2026

Last updated: 2026

Bounce & Break Blitz (the "Game") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the Game does and does not handle.

We do not collect any personal data

The Game does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, or any account.

No internet, ads, or analytics

The Game runs entirely offline. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and makes no network requests. No third parties receive any information from the Game.

Local data only

Your progress (high scores, coins, upgrades, settings, daily streak) is saved only on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the Game removes this local data.

Children

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
