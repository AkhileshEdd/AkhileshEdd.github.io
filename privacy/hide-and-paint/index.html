Hide And Paint! privacy policy

Effective 4 September 2026

Hide And Paint! needs an internet connection for online play. We don't ask for an account or email, and we only process what is needed to run a match.

Who we are

Hide And Paint! (the “game”) is published on Google Play by 100 ONE, the developer account of Akhilesh Mahto (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game handles and your choices. It applies only to Hide And Paint! (package com.akhilesh.hideandpaint).

Information we handle

The game saves the following on your device so it can work:

  • the player name you choose
  • settings and controls

These are saved on your phone and deleted when you uninstall the game. Your player name is also sent to the game server when you join a room, as described under Online play.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

Online play

To run a match, the game connects to a game server. The server receives the player name you chose, your in-game position and actions, and your device's IP address, which is needed to deliver the connection. This information is used only to run the room you are in.

There is no account, email or login. Your player name is visible to other players in the same room.

Voice and text chat

Voice chat is push-to-talk. While you hold the button, your voice is streamed in real time to other players in your room and fades with distance. Text chat messages are delivered to players in the room. We do not save recordings of voice chat.

Please don't share personal information in chat. If another player behaves badly, email us with the room details and we will look into it.

Permissions

  • Internet. Required to join online rooms and play with other people.
  • Microphone (optional). Used only while you hold push-to-talk, so other players in your room can hear you. The game is fully playable if you deny it.

Third parties

Hide And Paint! contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking libraries, and we don't share any information with third parties.

Children

Hide And Paint! is suitable for a general audience and is not directed primarily at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the game saves lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the game's storage in Android settings or uninstalling it.

If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to help you. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email akhileshedd@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

We keep the game small and limit what it can access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but because we don't collect personal information, there is very little that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Hide And Paint! handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's Google Play listing.
