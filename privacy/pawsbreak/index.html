Privacy policies / PawsBreak

PawsBreak privacy policy

Effective 26 October 2026

PawsBreak works offline. It does not collect, send or share any personal information.

Who we are

PawsBreak (the “app”) is published on Google Play by 100 ONE, the developer account of Akhilesh Mahto (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and your choices. It applies only to PawsBreak (package com.akhileshedd.pawsbreak).

Information we handle

The app saves the following on your device so it can work:

  • reminder schedule and quiet hours
  • chosen companion and popup style

This information stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

PawsBreak does not need the internet to work and does not request internet permission, so it can't send anything off your device.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps. Lets the companion float on top of other apps so it can show your reminders.
  • Notifications and foreground service. Shows one small ongoing notification while reminders are running, so Android keeps the reminder timer alive.

Third parties

PawsBreak contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking libraries, and we don't share any information with third parties.

Children

PawsBreak is suitable for a general audience and is not directed primarily at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app saves lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling it.

If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to help you. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email akhileshedd@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

We keep the app small and limit what it can access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but because we don't collect personal information, there is very little that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how PawsBreak handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing.
