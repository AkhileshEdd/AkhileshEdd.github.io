Cozy Mini Restaurant

Casual game for Android

Cook, serve cute animal customers and decorate a café run by Chef Mochi the cat.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy

Brew lattes, bake strawberry cakes and flip pancakes, then serve each order before your guests lose patience. Fast service means bigger tips.

Get to know your regulars, decorate the café your way, hire helpers and grow from a tiny kiosk into the town's favourite café.

What's inside

  • Recipes, kitchen stations and upgrades to unlock
  • Animal customers with their own stories
  • Decorate the café and dress up Chef Mochi
  • Seasons, rush hours and idle earnings
  • Plays offline, with no energy limits
