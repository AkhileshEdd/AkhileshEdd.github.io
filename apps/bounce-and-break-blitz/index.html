Bounce & Break Blitz

Casual game for Android

Aim, fire a stream of balls and smash the bricks before they reach the line.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy

Pull back to aim with a live trajectory preview, then release to launch a whole stream of bouncing balls at the numbered bricks.

Grab +1 tokens to grow your stream, chain combos, and watch out for void bricks, regenerators, movers and bosses.

What's inside

  • Classic, Boss Rush, Time Attack and Daily Challenge modes
  • Upgrades and colour themes bought with in-game coins
  • One-thumb controls
  • Plays fully offline
