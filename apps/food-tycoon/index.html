Food Tycoon

Simulation game for Android · Paid

A relaxed pixel-art idle tycoon: start with a lemonade stand and grow restaurants across eight cities.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy

Tap stations to cook and serve, collect your earnings and expand. Each of the eight cities has its own food style, from New York burgers and Tokyo sushi to Mumbai curries and Seoul BBQ.

Your restaurants keep earning while you're away. Hire managers, unlock research, prestige for permanent boosts and collect every recipe. Sessions can be short or long.

What's inside

  • Eight cities, each with its own menu and look
  • Offline earnings while you're away
  • Managers, upgrades, research and prestige resets
  • Achievements and recipe collection
  • Automatic saving
