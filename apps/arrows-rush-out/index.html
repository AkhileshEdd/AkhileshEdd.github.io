Arrows Rush Out

Puzzle game for Android

Tap the arrows out in the right order. A calm, offline puzzle.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy

Every arrow on the board can rush out, but only if its path is clear. Work out the order, untangle the board and clear every arrow without a crash.

No timers and no sign-up. Play one quick board on a break or sink into a dense maze.

What's inside

  • 100+ hand-tuned levels across Normal, Hard and Super Hard
  • Picture levels where whole shapes are built out of arrows
  • Five colour themes and optional grid lines
  • Hints when you are stuck
  • Plays fully offline
