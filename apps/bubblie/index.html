Bubblie

Parenting app for Android · Paid

Eleven gentle play screens for babies, locked so only a grown-up can leave.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy

Pick a screen, set a timer and hand over the phone. Back gestures stop working, the navigation bar hides and the volume can't go past the limit you set. To unlock, press and hold the bottom-left corner and enter your PIN.

While your little one plays, you can log a feed, a nappy change or a nap in two taps. Bubblie has no internet permission at all, so nothing can leave your phone.

What's inside

  • Eleven screens, grouped for 6–12 months and 1–3 years
  • Volume cap built into the sound itself
  • Two-tap care log and milestones
  • Weekly view of real screen time
  • No internet, no ads, no purchases, no account
