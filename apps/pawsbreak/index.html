PawsBreak

Health & Fitness app for Android

A small companion on your screen that reminds you to drink water, stretch and rest your eyes.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy

A cartoon companion sits in the corner of your screen. When it's time for a break it grows and shows a friendly nudge: drink some water, take a short walk, rest your eyes or stretch.

Drag it anywhere, set quiet hours, and choose how often it checks in.

What's inside

  • Reminders every 20, 30, 45 or 60 minutes
  • Choose which reminders you want
  • Quiet hours for night time
  • Cat, dog, frog, sloth or bot companion
  • Works fully offline with no account
