Ascendify

Productivity app for Android

A minimalist habit tracker for building daily routines and keeping streaks.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy

Create the habits you want to keep, check them off each day and watch your streaks grow on the progress calendar.

Reminders keep you on track, and analytics show your completion rate and longest streaks over time.

What's inside

  • Simple daily habit tracking
  • Progress calendar and streaks
  • Custom reminders
  • Dark mode and colour themes, plus animated themes
  • Works offline
