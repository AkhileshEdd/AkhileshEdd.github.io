Wordbloom

Word game for Android

A cozy word search with 3,000 levels, daily puzzles and Pip the fox.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy

Swipe across the letters to find hidden words that run across, down, diagonally and backwards. Travel through 120 themed categories, from Food and Animals to Space and Music.

Collect stars to open new worlds, find bonus and golden words, and dress up Pip along the way.

What's inside

  • 3,000 levels in 120 categories and 12 worlds
  • Adventure, Daily Challenge, Relaxed, Word Rush and Mystery modes
  • Hint, Rocket and Twist boosters
  • Reduced-motion and high-contrast options
  • Plays offline
