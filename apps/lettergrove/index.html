LetterGrove - Word Connect

Word game for Android

A calm word game: swipe across a letter wheel to spell words and fill the crossword.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy

Swipe across the letter wheel to spell words that fill a crossword grid. Travel through about 2,400 crosswords across 24 chapters, from three-letter words up to seven, with six illustrated locations along the way.

Come back for the daily crossword, weekly rewards and streaks. Free to play with optional rewarded ads and no account, timers or lives.

What's inside

  • About 2,400 crosswords in 24 chapters
  • Daily crossword, weekly events and streak shields
  • Definitions and a personal word journal
  • Colour themes, scenes and letter-wheel styles
  • Large text, high contrast and reduced-motion options
  • Plays offline, in portrait or landscape
