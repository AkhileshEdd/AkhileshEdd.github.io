Hide And Paint!

Casual game for Android

Online hide-and-seek where you paint yourself to match the walls and hide in plain sight.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy

Each round splits players into hiders and hunters. Hiders pick a spot, sample the exact colour of the wall behind them, strike a pose and hold still. Then the hunters are let loose.

Hiders only score while a hunter can actually see them, so the real play is standing in the open and hoping nobody looks twice. This is an early-access release.

What's inside

  • Classic, Infection and Double modes
  • Five maps, with more coming as free downloads
  • Online rooms for 2–12 players
  • Proximity push-to-talk voice chat and text chat
  • No ads, no purchases, no account
